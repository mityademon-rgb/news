# First AI lesson illustrations

Original illustrations created with built-in image_gen (not CLI) for TIMECODE, 19 October 2026. Use case: illustration-story. Final assets are WebP optimized to 1400 pixels wide; original PNGs are preserved in the generating workspace. No supplied portrait used. No factual screenshot or measured result is simulated by these illustrations.

Shared art specification: sophisticated warm editorial narrative illustration for adult community arts teachers, expressive female teacher in mustard sweater and fictional friendly violet helper; tactile grain, coral, lilac, mint and cream; clear visual narrative legible on phones; no text, logos or labels.

- `story.webp`: two-part comic. An unspecified request leads to an absurd orchestra, lasers and flying acrobat; real paper-and-pencil constraints lead to an achievable workshop. A metaphor for communicating conditions.
- `workshop.webp`: adult workshop participants make and compare paper forms, use image cards, turn a dry topic into hands-on activity.
- `team.webp`: writing, drawing, film, sound and research represented by distinct workstations and characters. Different tasks need different capabilities.
- `home.webp`: everyday assistance with ingredients for dinner, packing for a trip and understanding the actual manual of an appliance. Illustrations are not operating instructions.
- `learning.webp`: library of varied examples, pattern connections and a newly composed result under supplied conditions. Explicitly a simplified metaphor, not an exact technical diagram.

The instructional explanations, accurate numbers, procedure and safety-relevant limitations are real selectable HTML text, not text baked into generated images.
